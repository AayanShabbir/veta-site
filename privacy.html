Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026 · Effective: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Veta system ("Veta", "we", "us") handles information when you use our websites, interfaces, and agent services (collectively, the "Services"). Veta is operated by an independent developer (the "Operator").

1. Summary

  • Veta is a personal agent fleet. The Services process information to plan, execute, and verify tasks on your behalf.
  • We do not sell personal information. We do not run advertising trackers.
  • Automation is bounded by human approval gates for consequential actions (payments, publishing, account changes).

2. Information We Process

  • Account & contact information — identifiers you provide when contacting us (e.g., email address).
  • Task data — instructions, documents, credentials-vault references, and outputs you direct the Services to handle.
  • Integration data — information from connected services you authorize (e.g., email, calendar, repositories) strictly to perform requested automations.
  • Operational logs — task records, evidence artifacts, and system health data used for verification and reliability.

3. How Information Is Used

  • To operate, verify, and improve the Services (planning, execution, review, filing).
  • To provide alerts, briefs, and reports you have configured.
  • To maintain security: audit trails, watchdog checks, and abuse prevention.

We do not use task data to train third-party foundation models. Where a subprocessor (e.g., a model provider) may retain data per its own terms, we route the minimum data necessary for the task and prefer providers offering zero-retention modes.

4. Legal Bases

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on: your consent (for optional integrations), performance of a contract with you (to deliver the Services), and legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, service improvement).

5. Sharing & Subprocessors

We share information only with: (a) model and infrastructure providers strictly to execute tasks; (b) connected services you authorize; (c) authorities where legally required. A current list of subprocessor categories is available on request.

6. Data Retention

Task records and logs are retained while needed for verification and audit, then deleted or anonymized. Integration data is processed transiently unless persistence is required for a configured automation (e.g., scheduled reports). You may request deletion at any time.

7. Security

We apply defense in depth: credential vaulting, least-privilege permissions, permission walls between agent sectors, human approval gates for consequential actions, and continuous watchdog monitoring. No system is perfectly secure; we encourage strong, unique credentials everywhere.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, or restrict processing of your personal information. To exercise them, contact us using the details below. We respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

9. Children

The Services are not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

10. Changes

We may update this Policy. Material changes will be announced on the Services at least 7 days before taking effect, with an updated "Last updated" date.
